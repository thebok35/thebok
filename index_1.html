Send your introduction and resume in English to thebok35@gmail.com. Dr. Kim will review your profile and respond with a personal video introduction within 24 hours. English communication is required for all correspondence.

Korean bank transfer and USD wire transfer are both accepted. An official receipt from our Korean-registered company is available upon request.

100% refund available before the first session. For the 3-month plan, a 50% refund is available within the first month. For the Lifetime Package, a full refund is available before the 3rd session.

No Korean required. Sessions are in English and Indonesian. You will also learn essential Korean maritime vocabulary to help you communicate confidently on board.
